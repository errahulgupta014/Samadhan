HOW IT WORKS

From a problem to a resolution, in three steps

Every complaint gets a number, an owner and a visible history.

1

Report

Choose the department and category, add a photo, confirm the location and submit. It takes about a minute.

2

Track

The complaint is routed to the right department. Residents see each status change and receive updates in the app.

3

Resolve

When the work is done, a one-time code confirms the resolution before the complaint is closed.

FOR WARD OFFICES

A portal built for accountable service

Give every complaint an owner, and every decision a record.

Dashboards and reports

Filter by date, ward, department, category and status. Generate reports and export them to CSV, Excel, PDF or JSON.

Departments and categories

Each category belongs to a department, so new complaints reach the right contact person automatically.

Content you control

Publish notices, classifieds, activities and city information from one place, with banners and app settings.

Roles and permissions

Create admin users with exactly the access they need. Only a Super Admin can delete records, and every delete asks first.
